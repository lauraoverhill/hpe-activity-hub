Why does the graph go down at the end?

After 2017, new rules reduced how many opioids doctors prescribed, and harms began to fall. Fewer prescriptions in the community meant fewer chances for misuse, dependence and overdose — so the number of deaths started to drop. This is an example of supply reduction and harm minimisation working together.

Source of real figures: Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, Opioid harm in Australia and PBS data. Teachers: swap in the latest AIHW figures any time.
