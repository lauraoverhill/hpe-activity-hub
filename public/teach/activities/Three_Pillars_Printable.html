Word check
  • Harm reduction — Reduces harm for people who do use — e.g. pill testing, needle programs, safe spaces.
  • Demand reduction — Reduces how many people want to use — e.g. education, treatment, rehab.
  • Supply reduction — Reduces the drugs available — e.g. police, customs, laws, prisons.
