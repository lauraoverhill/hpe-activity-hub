Respond to a Friend

Name: _____________________________    Class: ____________    Date: ____________

Message from Sam:
“I've been taking codeine for my sore back. Lately I think I need more than I used to, and I feel weird when I stop. I'm a bit scared. What do I do?”

Write your reply to Sam. A good reply is caring, accurate, and points to real help. The sentence starters below can help you.

Sentence starters (word bank):
1. Acknowledge how Sam feels
  • Thanks for trusting me with this.
  • That sounds really stressful — I'm glad you told me.
  • It makes sense that you feel scared.
2. Share an accurate fact
  • Needing more over time is called tolerance, and it's common with codeine.
  • Feeling weird when you stop can be a sign of dependence — and it can be treated.
  • The good news is that support for this really works.
3. Point to real help
  • A pharmacist can give free, private advice.
  • Health Direct and the ADF have reliable information.
  • Talking to your GP or a trusted adult is a good next step.
4. Offer your support
  • You're not alone — I'm here for you.
  • We could look at Health Direct together if you like.
  • I can come with you to talk to someone.
