Mix & Match: Key Words

Name: _____________________________    Class: ____________    Date: ____________

Write the letter of the matching meaning in the blank next to each key word.

Key words

  1. Drug  —  ____
  2. Stimulant  —  ____
  3. Depressant  —  ____
  4. Hallucinogen  —  ____
  5. Opioid  —  ____
  6. Legal  —  ____
  7. Illegal  —  ____
  8. Illicit  —  ____

Meanings

  1. A strong painkiller drug that slows the body down and can be very addictive.
  2. Against the law — for example, selling heroin.
  3. Allowed by law — for example, caffeine in a soft drink.
  4. Any substance that changes how your body or mind works.
  5. Changes what a person sees, hears or feels, and can cause hallucinations.
  6. Slows down the messages between the brain and body — you might feel relaxed or sleepy.
  7. Speeds up the messages between the brain and body — you might feel more awake or alert.
  8. Using a drug in a way that breaks the rules or law — even a legal one, like misusing a prescription medicine.
