Year 8 Health · Lesson 13 · Printable worksheet

Fact Finder

Name: _____________________________    Class: ____________    Date: ____________

  1. From Health Direct:
    “Opioids are a class of drugs that slow the central nervous system and are often used to treat pain.”
    Circle the best paraphrase (accurate and in your own words):
    A. Opioids slow down the body's nervous system and are often used to help with pain.
    B. Opioids are a class of drugs that slow the central nervous system and are often used to treat pain.
    C. Opioids speed up the nervous system and give people energy.
  2. From Alcohol and Drug Foundation:
    “Dependence is when a person's body or mind starts to feel like it needs a drug to function normally.”
    Circle the best paraphrase (accurate and in your own words):
    A. Dependence is when a person's body or mind starts to feel like it needs a drug to function normally.
    B. Dependence is when your body or mind feels like it needs a drug just to feel normal.
    C. Dependence means a person has completely stopped using a drug.
  3. From Alcohol and Drug Foundation:
    “Mixing depressant drugs, such as alcohol and benzodiazepines, increases the risk of harm because their effects add together.”
    Circle the best paraphrase (accurate and in your own words):
    A. Mixing depressants like alcohol and benzos is safe because their effects cancel out.
    B. Mixing depressant drugs, such as alcohol and benzodiazepines, increases the risk of harm because their effects add together.
    C. Taking two depressants together, like alcohol and benzos, is more dangerous because their effects stack up.
  4. From NPS MedicineWise:
    “Pharmacists can give advice about medicines and how to take them safely.”
    Circle the best paraphrase (accurate and in your own words):
    A. Pharmacists can help you understand your medicine and how to take it safely.
    B. Pharmacists only sell medicines and can't give any advice.
    C. Pharmacists can give advice about medicines and how to take them safely.
