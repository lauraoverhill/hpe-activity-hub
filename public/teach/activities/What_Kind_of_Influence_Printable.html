Year 8 Health · Lesson 3 · Printable worksheet

What Kind of Influence?

Name: _____________________________    Class: ____________    Date: ____________

Word check
  • Social — The people around you — friends, family, peer pressure.
  • Emotional — Your feelings — stress, wanting to belong, boredom.
  • Environmental — Your surroundings — what's available, where you are, what you see.

Circle whether each example is a social, emotional, or environmental influence.

  1. A friend offers you a drink at a party.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  2. You keep seeing vaping in ads and on social media.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  3. You want to feel more confident and less shy.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  4. There are no adults around at the party.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  5. Your friends dare you to try something.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  6. You feel really stressed and want to escape for a while.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  7. Drugs are cheap and easy to get where you live.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  8. Your older sibling uses drugs, so it feels normal to you.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental
  9. You're bored and looking for a thrill.
    SocialEmotionalEnvironmental

Answer key

#StatementAnswerWhy
1A friend offers you a drink at a party.SocialA friend offering you something is pressure from other people.
2You keep seeing vaping in ads and on social media.EnvironmentalAds and social media are things in your environment.
3You want to feel more confident and less shy.EmotionalWanting to change how you feel is an emotional influence.
4There are no adults around at the party.EnvironmentalWhether adults are around is about the setting you're in.
5Your friends dare you to try something.SocialA dare from friends is about the people around you.
6You feel really stressed and want to escape for a while.EmotionalWanting to escape a feeling like stress is an emotional influence.
7Drugs are cheap and easy to get where you live.EnvironmentalEasy access is about your surroundings.
8Your older sibling uses drugs, so it feels normal to you.SocialFamily behaviour that makes it seem normal is a social influence.
9You're bored and looking for a thrill.EmotionalChasing a thrill out of boredom is driven by feelings.
