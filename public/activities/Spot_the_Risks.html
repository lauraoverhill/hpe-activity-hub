Spot the Risks

When someone uses drugs or alcohol, the risks go well beyond the body. Read each risk from a party scenario and sort it into the right type.

PhysicalHarm to your body — injury, illness, passing out.
SocialHarm to friendships and reputation — losing trust, embarrassment.
EmotionalHarm to your feelings — stress, guilt, regret, worry.
LegalTrouble with the law — fines, police, a criminal record.
