Legal, Illegal, or Illicit?

Read each short scenario and decide how the law treats that drug use. Some scenarios involve a legal drug being misused — that makes them illicit, not just legal or illegal.

LegalAllowed by law for that person's age — e.g. an adult drinking alcohol, or anyone drinking caffeine.
IllegalAgainst the law to possess or use, for anyone, in any circumstance — e.g. cocaine, heroin, MDMA.
IllicitIllegal use of a drug that would otherwise be legal — e.g. underage drinking, or misusing a prescription medicine.
