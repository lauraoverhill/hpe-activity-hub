The Three Pillars

Australia's approach to drugs is called harm minimisation, and it has three pillars. Sort each real example into the pillar it belongs to.

Harm reductionReduces harm for people who do use — e.g. pill testing, needle programs, safe spaces.
Demand reductionReduces how many people want to use — e.g. education, treatment, rehab.
Supply reductionReduces the drugs available — e.g. police, customs, laws, prisons.
